# ELI Group — Investor Reel

A self-playing, looping highlight reel of ELI Group's work — from land preparation to export — built for display at investor meetings and booths.

**Live:** https://yamkeladev.github.io/Eli-reel-/

## What it shows

- **Who we are** — ELI Group, ELI Foods, KSI (KwaZulu-Natal Spice Initiative), and West Coast Spice Initiative
- **01 · Land Preparation**
- **02 · Training & Skills Development** — 1000+ people trained
- **03 · Planting** — 1200+ farmers growing with us
- **04 · Harvesting** — 3000+ jobs created
- **05 · Logistics & Transport**
- **06 · Market, Processing & Export**

## How to use it

Open the link above on any screen, TV, or laptop browser and go fullscreen (`F11` in most browsers). It autoplays and loops on its own — no clicking, no remote, no narration needed. Built for a few minutes of walk-by viewing: stop for 10 seconds or the full loop, either way tells the story.

## Tech notes

A single self-contained `index.html` — no build step, no external files, no dependencies. All photos and logos are embedded directly in the page, so it runs identically wherever it's hosted.
